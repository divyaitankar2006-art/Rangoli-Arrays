How to Play Rangoli Arrays

🌸
1

Understand the Array

An array has Rows (across ↔) and Columns (down ↕).
For example, 3 rows of 4 dots makes 3 × 4 = 12 dots!

2

Adjust Rows & Columns

Use the big + and − buttons to set your rows and columns to match the question.

3

Colour with Rangoli Powder

Pick your favourite vibrant colour powder and tap each dot to fill it in beautifully.

4

Check & Celebrate!

Tap Check Answer to verify! Earn shiny gold stars, hear cheerful praises, and unlock higher challenges.
